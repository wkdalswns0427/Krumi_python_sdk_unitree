CONFIDENT: standard references
=================================================================

[1]  A. Liegeois, "Automatic supervisory control of the configuration and
     behavior of multibody mechanisms," IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and
     Cybernetics, vol. 7, no. 12, pp. 868-871, 1977.
     Use for: null-space joint-limit avoidance by gradient projection, the
     standard remedy the paper measures against.

[2]  T. Yoshikawa, "Manipulability of robotic mechanisms," The International
     Journal of Robotics Research, vol. 4, no. 2, pp. 3-9, 1985.
     Use for: the manipulability measure reported as a conditioning metric.

[3]  Y. Nakamura and H. Hanafusa, "Inverse kinematic solutions with singularity
     robustness for robot manipulator control," Journal of Dynamic Systems,
     Measurement, and Control, vol. 108, no. 3, pp. 163-171, 1986.
     Use for: damped least squares, the B2 baseline solver.

[4]  C. W. Wampler, "Manipulator inverse kinematic solutions based on vector
     formulations and damped least-squares methods," IEEE Transactions on
     Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, vol. 16, no. 1, pp. 93-101, 1986.
     Use for: damped least squares, cite alongside [3].

[5]  D. E. Whitney, "Resolved motion rate control of manipulators and human
     prostheses," IEEE Transactions on Man-Machine Systems, vol. 10, no. 2,
     pp. 47-53, 1969.
     Use for: the origin of resolved-rate redundancy resolution.

[6]  L. McAtamney and E. N. Corlett, "RULA: a survey method for the
     investigation of work-related upper limb disorders," Applied Ergonomics,
     vol. 24, no. 2, pp. 91-99, 1993.
     Use for: the posture score in X7 and the replica-versus-replacement
     argument.

[7]  S. Hignett and L. McAtamney, "Rapid Entire Body Assessment (REBA),"
     Applied Ergonomics, vol. 31, no. 2, pp. 201-205, 2000.
     Use for: whole-body posture scoring, cite alongside [6].

[8]  G. Casiez, N. Roussel, and D. Vogel, "1 euro filter: a simple speed-based
     low-pass filter for noisy input in interactive systems," in Proc. SIGCHI
     Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI), 2012,
     pp. 2527-2530.
     Use for: the direction-conditioning filter in the methods section. This
     citation is now load-bearing, since omitting the filter changed our
     conclusions.

[9]  M. Loper, N. Mahmood, J. Romero, G. Pons-Moll, and M. J. Black, "SMPL: a
     skinned multi-person linear model," ACM Transactions on Graphics, vol. 34,
     no. 6, pp. 248:1-248:16, 2015.
     Use for: the body model that the hard-keypoint baseline fits, which we
     reproduce in closed form from the robot's link lengths.
